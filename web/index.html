Set it up

      
    
        
  1. Fit the unit. Stick the case in the glove box with Velcro. Any position works, but it must not slide or tumble.
    2. 
        
  2. Power it. Use a phone charger in a socket that turns off with the ignition, not a screen's USB port, and start the car. One long beep means no beacon is paired yet.
    3. 
        
  3. Start the hotspot. Triple-click the button on top of the case. You hear three short beeps. It does not start by itself.
    4. 
        
  4. Join it. In your phone's Wi-Fi settings, choose YogiGate-XXXX. The password is password. If the phone warns about no internet, stay connected.
    5. 
        
  5. Open this page. Go to http://192.168.4.1, and type the http:// too.
    6. 
        
  6. Pair the beacon. On the Beacon tab, either type its MAC, or tap Scan and then Use. At the bench, you can also hold the button for 3 s and hold the beacon against the unit. You hear short, short, long.
    7. 
        
  7. Test it, parked. On the Status tab, Signal shows a number and Live motion moves when you shake the unit. Close the glove box and tap Press the remote. You hear one short beep, and the gate must open from the parking spot.
    8. 
        
  8. Test it, driving. Start the car and wait: the gate must stay shut. Drive off: the gate opens within a few seconds, with no beep.
    9. 
      

    

    

      
What each sound means

      
No beep when the gate opens by itself. That is correct.

      
One short: you pressed the remote by hand

      
Two short: pairing started, so hold the beacon close

      
Three short: the hotspot is starting

      
Short, short, long: success, saved

      
Two rattles: that did not work, so try again

      
One long at power-on: no beacon is paired yet

      
Three long: the motion sensor failed

      
Five short, one long: factory reset, erasing

    

    

      
The button

      
One click: press the remote

      
Two clicks: set the set point here, from the current signal

      
Triple click: start the hotspot

      
Hold 3 s: pair a beacon held close

      
Hold 15 s: factory reset. It erases the settings, beacon and logs, and puts the password back to password. With the unit on, hold the button past the two short beeps, until you hear five short and one long. One long beep means it is done, within a minute

    

    

      
If it goes wrong

      
Page will not open: join by hand in Wi-Fi settings, and type http://

      
Gate stays shut: check the remote's battery and range, then the Signal on the Status tab

      
Opens from the street: raise the set point on the Settings tab

      
Opens while parked: fix the unit so it cannot move, then rate that trip on the Logs tab

      
Three long beeps: check the sensor's four wires. Until fixed, the gate can open from the spot

      
Remote's LED stays on: its two wires are swapped

    

    

      
Good to know

      
Replace the remote's battery every year, because the unit cannot tell when it dies. Advanced mode, at the bottom of the Settings tab, shows every setting, Reset to defaults, Reset logs, Factory reset and firmware upload.
